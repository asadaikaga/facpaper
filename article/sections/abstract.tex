We consider linear systems of the form $AB\boldsymbol{x}=\boldsymbol{b}$. To solve linear systems of this type, we consider three approaches. The first one solves the linear system by the usual Krylov Subspace method; the second one splits the linear system into two subsystems; and the third one solves an equivalent augmented system. We give a theoretical analysis for the second approach and then propose our stopping critereia for the approach, which is motivated by [K. Du, Appl. Math.
Comput. 466 (2024) 128648]. The stopping criteria is then verified in the first numerical experiment. Further, we conduct numerical experiments to compare the three approaches for large and sparse matrices in the second numerical experiment.
